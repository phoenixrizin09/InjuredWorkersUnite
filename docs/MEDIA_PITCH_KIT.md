## 🎤 INTERVIEW PREP (If They Want to Interview You)

### Common Questions & Answers

**Q: "Why should people trust you? You're not a lawyer or scientist."**

A: "Exactly. That's the point. I'm an injured worker like the people we serve. 
I'm not trying to be an expert. I'm just sharing government documents that are 
already public but hard to find. The evidence speaks for itself—it's all verifiable."

**Q: "Isn't this just one person's opinion?"**

A: "No, this is statistical analysis of government data. We're not making claims. 
We're presenting what WSIB itself published: 61% denial rate for chronic pain vs 28% 
for physical injuries. That gap requires explanation."

**Q: "Have you talked to WSIB about this?"**

A: "We've requested official response multiple times. [Include what happened]. 
We're not trying to gotcha them—we want their explanation. The data is the data."

**Q: "What do you want people to do with this information?"**

A: "Appeal if they've been wrongfully denied. Use evidence instead of emotion. 
Get community support. Some will win—many already have. Some won't. But at least 
they'll know they weren't alone and there's a strategy."

**Q: "Are you taking money for this? Funded by anyone?"**

A: "Zero profit. Everything is free. We're not taking money from anyone. This 
is volunteer-run. I'm disabled and can't work full-time so I built tools that help 
people in my situation."
