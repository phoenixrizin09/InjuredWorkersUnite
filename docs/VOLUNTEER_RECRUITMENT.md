# 🤝 VOLUNTEER RECRUITMENT SYSTEM - Scripts & Templates

## OVERVIEW

Three types of volunteers:
1. **Sharers** (Zero effort) - Just share posts
2. **Organizers** (2 hours/month) - Run city groups
3. **Contributors** (5-10 hrs/week) - Help build tools

This document includes scripts, forms, and onboarding for all three.

---

## 🎯 TIER 1: SHARERS (The "Do Nothing" Volunteers)

### Why Recruit Sharers?
- No training needed
- Reach exponentially increases
- They recruit other sharers organically
- Cost: $0, effort: minimal, impact: massive

### Sharer Recruitment Message

**On homepage banner:**

```
WANT TO HELP?

You don't have to do anything.
We do the research. You just share.

No meetings. No commitments. No energy required.

[BUTTON: "Make me a sharer"]
```

**In email to new signups:**

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for joining. You now get our daily briefings.

But here's a secret: You can be more helpful by just doing one thing.

Share.

When we send something that resonates with you, share it to your network.
Tweet it. Post it. Text the link to a friend. 

That's it.

You probably know 5-10 people dealing with benefit denials.
Every time you share, one of them might find us.

One share = One person gets hope = One person wins an appeal = One family 
stays housed.

You don't need to become an activist.
You don't need to read everything.
You don't need to understand the legal stuff.

Just share when you can.

[BUTTON: "I'll share your stuff"]

Thanks,
The Eye Oracle Team
```

- Builds face-to-face community
- Politicians respond to local pressure
- Creates recurring revenue (donations) for movement

BUT we cover:
✓ Zoom (pro account on us)
✓ Printing (if you want flyers - we send PDFs)

---

COMMON QUESTIONS:

Q: "What if nobody shows up to my first call?"
A: Start with 5-10. That's plenty. Quality > quantity.

Q: "What if I mess up?"
A: You won't. We're supporting you. And "mistakes" in organizing 
   build trust (shows you're real).

Q: "Can I step back if it gets overwhelming?"
A: Yes. Tell us. We'll find someone else. No shame.

Q: "Do I need to be super knowledgeable?"
A: No. You're a facilitator, not a teacher. We provide all the info.

---

NEXT STEPS:

1. Confirm: You good to start?
2. We'll: Send Zoom link + discussion guide
3. You: Pick first meeting date
4. We'll: Send reminder + attendee list
5. Go: Have your first call!

[BUTTON: "I'm ready - send me the stuff"]

The Eye Oracle Team
```

### Contributor Onboarding Call

**Schedule 30-min video call within 24 hours of application**

**Call script:**

```
"Hi [Name], thanks for applying. I'm pumped.

Quick questions to get you started:

1. What's your specific skill? How long have you been doing it?

2. How many hours per week can you commit? (Real honest answer)

3. What impact do you want to have? 
   (Help injured workers? Change the system? Something else?)

4. Are you injured/disabled? (Doesn't matter, just curious about perspective)

5. What would make this work feel meaningful to you?

[Listen to answers]

Here's what I'm thinking:

[Propose specific project based on their skill]

This would take about [X hours] and would directly help [Y number] of people.

Does that sound interesting?

[If yes]:

Great. Here's what happens next:

- I send you all the info/tools you need tonight
- We have weekly check-ins (15 min, optional)
- You work on your own time
- First month is trial—if it's too much or not working, no problem

Any questions before we start?"

## 🎯 VOLUNTEER RETENTION SYSTEM

### Red Flags (Watch For)

- Volunteer goes quiet for 2 weeks → Reach out
- Volunteer says "too much" → Reduce commitment
- Volunteer seems burned out → Offer break

### How to Save a Volunteer

**Email template if they go quiet:**

```
Hi [Name],

Haven't heard from you in a bit. Checking in.

No judgment—life happens. Disability is unpredictable.

Quick options:

[ ] I'm still in but busy—keep me in loop, I'll jump back in when able
[ ] I'm overwhelmed—let's reduce commitment to [X] 
[ ] I need a break—just pause and pick up when ready
[ ] Something else [explain below]

Just let me know. We're good either way.

No hard feelings. This is volunteer work. You don't owe us anything.

[Your name]
```

## 🎁 VOLUNTEER INCENTIVES (Optional)

**What actually motivates volunteers in disability justice movement:**

1. **Seeing impact** (most important)
   - Share stories: "Because you shared, [person] won"
   - Monthly reports: "Your 4 shares reached 2,400 people"

2. **Community**
   - Monthly volunteer calls
   - Discord/Slack for volunteers
   - Cross-city collaboration

3. **Autonomy**
   - Pick your own hours
   - Choose your projects
   - Set your own pace

4. **Recognition**
   - Feature volunteer of the month
   - Credit them publicly
   - Thank them consistently

5. **Free stuff** (low priority, but nice)
   - Merch (sticker, shirt) if budget allows
   - Free access to premium resources
