## 💡 INSIDER TIPS

1. **Post consistency matters more than perfection**
   - Post every day > perfect weekly post
   - Authentic > polished
   - Real stories > marketing language

2. **Reply to EVERY comment in first 24 hours**
   - Algorithm rewards engagement
   - People feel heard = more action
   - Community builds reciprocal support

3. **Email open rates improve with testing**
   - Test subject lines (2 versions, pick winner)
   - Send at different times (track which time performs)
   - Make content shorter (disability audience prefers concise)

4. **Journalists want evidence packets**
   - Provide everything ready to go
   - Interview-ready victims (anonymized intro)
   - Media releases pre-written
   - They just need to publish

5. **Volunteers feel valued when recognized**
   - Feature volunteer of the week
   - Show impact of their shares
   - Send appreciation emails monthly
   - Report on victories they enabled

6. **Growth compounds after Month 2**
   - Month 1: You're building foundation
   - Month 2: You get first wins + media
   - Month 3+: Network effects take over
