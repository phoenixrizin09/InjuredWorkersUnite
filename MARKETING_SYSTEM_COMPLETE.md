## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Too much, too fast**
- Don't try to do everything Week 1
- Follow the playbook sequentially
- One thing at a time

❌ **Inconsistency**
- Post every day (consistency > perfection)
- Reply to comments reliably
- Send emails on schedule

❌ **Complicated systems**
- Don't build custom tools
- Use templates as-is
- Don't optimize before launching

❌ **Giving up too early**
- Week 1 will feel slow
- Week 2-3 momentum builds
- Week 4 you see compound growth

❌ **Doing it all yourself**
- Recruit volunteers immediately
- Delegate what you can
- Preserve your energy

## 🆘 WHEN TO PIVOT

**If email open rate < 20%:**
- Try new subject line
- Shorten email content
- Change send time

**If no social engagement:**
- Test different content type
- Ask questions in posts
- Post at different times
- Add images/memes

**If no volunteer signups:**
- Make ask more specific
- Lower barrier to entry
- Feature early volunteers
- Be more visible about need

**If too overwhelmed:**
- Reduce posting frequency
- Delegate to volunteer
- Focus on email only
- Take a week off
